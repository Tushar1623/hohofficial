## Brand & Style

This design system channels the electrifying, high-stakes atmosphere of India's marquee stand-up comedy talent arena. Built around the physical sensation of stepping onto a darkened comedy club stage under blazing spotlights, the aesthetic fuses **cinematic stage drama** with **bold editorial entertainment modernism**.

The target audience spans aspiring comics, nationwide comedy aficionados, judges, and broadcast viewers tuning into high-voltage digital streams, open-mic audition submissions, and national voting rounds. The interface evokes anticipation, electric excitement, raw comedic edge, and world-class theatrical production. Deep, pitch-black backdrops frame vibrant bursts of stage amber, radiant spotlight gold, and dramatic theater-curtain crimson. High-contrast typography cuts through the shadows with punchy, uninhibited presence.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict dark mode, establishing an authentic stage environment where light is treated as an active directional element:

- **Primary (`#FF8A00`)**: Energetic Stage Orange. Used for hero calls-to-action, active voting buttons, live stage indicators, and focal interactive elements.
- **Secondary (`#FFB000`)**: Warm Spotlight Yellow. Represents the focused beam of light above the comic—used for leaderboards, winner highlights, badges, and glowing interactive hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#E53935`)**: Crimson Stage Curtain. Used for urgency markers, "LIVE" ribbons, elimination markers, regional zones, and high-impact event tags.
- **Neutral & Surfaces**:
  - `Canvas Deep Black`: `#050505` (the outer darkness of an auditorium).
  - `Surface Tier 1`: `#0D0D0D` (panel backgrounds, cards, and modal canvases).
  - `Surface Tier 2`: `#141414` (elevated cards, video modules, and form inputs).
  - `Surface Tier 3`: `#1F1F1F` (hover states, dropdown menus, and nested chips).
- **Text & Accents**:
  - `Crisp White`: `#F5F5F5` (headings, high-priority counts, and prominent labels).
  - `Muted Stage Gray`: `#A0A0A0` (body copy, timestamps, secondary bios, and metadata).
  - `Subtle Border`: `#262626` (structured, unobtrusive hairline dividers).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes bold entertainment poster traditions against sharp, legible digital product readability:

- **Display & Headlines (`Bebas Neue`)**: Tall, condensed, and assertive. Used in all-caps for show titles, talent names, scores, leaderboard standing, and promotional banners to channel classic comedy club marquees and arena poster title treatments.
- **Body (`Outfit`)**: Clean, modern, and geometrically warm sans-serif. Delivers exceptional readability across comic audition entries, judge critiques, rules, and user profiles without competing against display titles.
- **Labels & Badges (`Space Grotesk`)**: Monospaced-leaning geometric sans-serif with subtle technical precision. Used for regional indicators (`NORTH`, `SOUTH`, `EAST`, `WEST`), live voting tallies, round timestamps, and category tags. Always capitalized with expanded letter-spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed for heavy media density: audition clips, performer rosters, live streaming stage feeds, and voting brackets.

- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Mobile` (< 768px): 4 columns, `margin-mobile` of `1rem`, `gutter-mobile` of `0.75rem`.
  - `Tablet` (768px – 1024px): 8 columns, margin of `2rem`, gutter of `1rem`.
  - `Desktop` (> 1024px): 12 columns, max-width `1440px` centered canvas, outer margin of `3rem`, gutter of `1.25rem`.
- **Rhythm**: Spacing follows an 8pt baseline rhythm. Component interiors use compact padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to keep visual tension high, while section modules employ generous separation (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`) allowing spotlight hero imagery to command the stage.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **theatrical spotlighting and dark tonal stacking**, avoiding generic grey drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Stage Void)**: Background canvas `#050505` with optional subtle radial spotlight gradients (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.12) 0%, transparent 70%)`).
- **Level 1 (Card Deck)**: Surface `#0D0D0D`, bordered with a fine `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover Card)**: Surface `#141414`, bordered with `1px solid rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.35)`, lifted by an energetic halo: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.18)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Standout Spotlight)**: Surface `#1F1F1F`, bordered with `1px solid rgba(255, 176, 0, 0.5)`, casting a high-intensity spotlight glow: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(255, 176, 0, 0.28)`.
- **Theatrical Spotlights**: Key talent cards and hero banners feature an under-lit cone effect created using top radial flares in `#FFB000` fading to `#050505`.

## Shapes

The design system relies on a **Soft / Architectural Sharpness** (`roundedness: 1`), embodying the crisp, punchy edge of stand-up comedy and live performance sets:

- Standard controls, audition cards, and input fields utilize `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii.
- Modals, large player containers, and spotlight talent cards use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Status tags and badge pills allow `rounded-full` strictly for small tags (e.g., `LIVE`, `ROUND 1`, `TOP 10`) to create instant visual contrast against structural card shapes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Stage Button**: Solid `#FF8A00` fill with `#050505` bold uppercase text (`Space Grotesk`, `label-lg`). Border radius 4px. On hover: shifts toward `#FFB000` with an amber spotlight glow `0 0 20px rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.5)`.
- **Secondary Crimson Action**: Solid `#E53935` fill, `#F5F5F5` text, used exclusively for urgent actions like "VOTE NOW" or "SUBMIT AUDITION".
- **Outline / Ghost Button**: Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` border, `#F5F5F5` text. On hover: border tints to `#FF8A00` with subtle surface fill `rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.08)`.

### Cards & Talent Profiles
- Constructed on `#0D0D0D` with top-weighted spotlight gradients.
- Includes comic performance thumbnail/video preview, performer stage name in `headline-lg` (`Bebas Neue`), regional indicator badge (`label-tag`), and audition score in spotlight gold (`#FFB000`).
- Card borders intensify to vibrant stage orange on hover with a smooth 200ms transition.

### Chips & Badges
- **Zone Badge**: Angular pill chip with `#141414` background, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` outline, and `Space Grotesk` uppercase text.
- **Live Tag**: Background `#E53935`, crisp white text, accompanied by an animated pulsating red spotlight dot indicator.

### Inputs & Search
- Surface `#141414` with a hairline `#262626` border and 4px corners. Text set in `#F5F5F5` with `#A0A0A0` placeholder.
- Focus state activates an intense `#FF8A00` border paired with an inner and outer amber glow (`0 0 0 1px #FF8A00`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square 4px boxes for checkboxes; circular rings for radios.
- Unchecked: `#141414` background with `#404040` border.
- Checked: `#FF8A00` background with dark `#050505` check icon or radio center point.

### Specialized Entertainment Modules
- **Audition Video Player**: Frame featuring high-contrast neon timestamps and live audience applause/laugh meter overlay in `#FFB000`.
- **Leaderboard Ranks**: Large `Bebas Neue` numbers (01, 02, 03) rendered in `#FFB000` with podium-tier framing, differentiating top qualifiers from the rest of the pack.
